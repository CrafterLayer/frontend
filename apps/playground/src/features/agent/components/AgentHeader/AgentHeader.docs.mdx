# AgentHeader

The agent panel's header (SPEC §4.2): one 44px line with أبو غضب's face, his name, and two actions,
Conversations and New conversation.

<Canvas of={AgentHeaderStories.AtRest} />

## Purpose

The header says whose panel this is and offers the two things you do to the conversation as a whole.
It says nothing else. The v1 header read "Agent Let m…", with a truncated conversation title and a
count badge beside it (the owner's screenshot): a status clipped to four letters, an English word
for an Arabic character, and a title nobody could read. Each of those has a better home now: the
run's status is the RunBar's line, the conversation's title is its row in Conversations, and
progress counts sit on the records.

## When to use / not

Use it once, as the first row of AgentPanel, and as the top of the phone sheet (AgentSheet passes
Close and Show the page). Do not add a badge, a count or a second name to it. Do not use it outside
the agent panel.

The Studio Indigo redesign (2026-10-12) adds one optional word-status under the name (`status`):
what the Agent is doing in a word or three («يبني الصفحة», "Waiting for your decision"), from
`headerStatusKey` in `model/header-status.ts`. It fits inside the same 44px (20 + 16), is drawn
text, never a live region, and carries no count, clock or title: those stay the RunBar's.

## States

- **At rest** (`AtRest`, `AtRestArabic`, `Dark`): flat, no line under it.
- **Scrolled** (`Scrolled`, `ScrolledArabicDark`): the thread is scrolled beneath it, so a 1px
  Hairline appears at its block end.
- **Running** (`Running`, `RunningArabic`, `DarkRunning`): the face works; New conversation stays in
  the tab order but is `aria-disabled`, and its tooltip's second line says "Available when the
  current work ends". A press does nothing.
- **Conversations open** (`ConversationsOpen`): `aria-expanded="true"`, the Wash stays under the
  button, and its tooltip steps aside for the popover.
- **Widths** (`Narrow288`, `Wide480Arabic`): under a 320px panel the gutter narrows to 12px and the
  face follows it.
- **Background jobs** (`WithBackgroundJobs`, `WithBackgroundJobsArabicDark`): the stacked-pages
  button after Skills & memory opens BackgroundJobsDialog (`aria-haspopup="dialog"`). A 6px dot at
  its corner says answers wait to be applied (Ink) or a batch still answers (Graphite); the button's
  second tooltip line, also its description, says how many. Never Indigo.
- **Phone sheet** (`PhoneSheet`, `PhoneSheetArabic`): Close, the face and the name; Conversations,
  New and Show the page. 56px tall (AgentSheet sets `--bldr-agent-header-height`), 44px targets 8px
  apart.

## Anatomy

```text
[16] face 24 [8] name (h2, one line …………)  [◷ 32] 4 [+ 32] [8]     44px, one line
[8] [× 44] face name ………………………  [◷ 44] 8 [+ 44] 8 [⤓ 44] [8]      phone sheet, 56px
```

- **Face**: `AgentFace size="sm"` (24px), `live`, its mood following the run. `aria-hidden`.
- **Name**: the panel's `h2`, Name type (14/20 Latin, 15/22 Arabic), 600, Ink. «أبو غضب» or "Abu
  Ghadab", in the UI language only. One line, clipped with an ellipsis at the inline end if a panel
  is ever too narrow for it.
- **Actions**: two ghost IconButtons (32px, 44px coarse), 4px apart, 8px apart on a coarse pointer.
- **Hairline**: an inset shadow in the header's last pixel row, so it moves nothing. It fades in
  over `state` (120ms), a colour change only.

## Accessibility

- The name is the panel's `h2`; AgentPanel names its region by it (`headingId`).
- The face is decoration; the header's text is the name and nothing else.
- Conversations is a dialog trigger: `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls`
  while open. HistoryPopover anchors to `conversationsRef` (Radix `Popover.Anchor virtualRef`) and
  returns focus to it when it closes (SPEC §4.18).
- New conversation is `aria-disabled` while a run is live, never `disabled`: it stays focusable, and
  its description says why and when.
- Each button's accessible name is its tooltip's text (IconButton).
- `prefers-contrast: more` draws the Hairline in Rule; forced colours draw it as a real border.

## RTL

Logical properties only. In Arabic the face and the name stand at the right, the actions at the left
(`AtRestArabic` measures it), and tooltips carry the panel's language and direction.

## Composition

```tsx
<AgentHeader
  locale={state.locale}
  face={mood}
  scrolled={threadScrolled}
  running={state.run !== null}
  conversationsOpen={historyOpen}
  onConversations={() => setHistoryOpen((open) => !open)}
  onNewConversation={controller.newConversation}
  conversationsRef={historyButton}
  conversationsControls={historyId}
  headingId={panelNameId}
/>
```

## Limitations

- The face's mood comes from its owner (`useAgentMood` in AgentPanel); the header does not compute
  it.
- HistoryPopover is its own component (WP-15). If it would rather wrap its trigger than anchor to
  it, the integration adapts through `conversationsRef`: the header owns the button.
