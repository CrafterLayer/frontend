# AgentThread

The conversation (SPEC §4.4, §3.12): the panel's middle row and its only scroller. It holds the
turns, the greeting when there are none, the suggestion rows, and the jump pill.

<Canvas of={AgentThreadStories.ScrolledUpBuilding} />

The stories draw the whole frame AgentPanel will: header, thread, a stand-in composer and the live
region. The records inside are sketches on the RunRecord, RunGlyph and ChangeLines primitives;
BuildRecord, EditRecord, UserMessage and PagePlanCard are their own work packages.

## Purpose

- **Read in peace.** The thread follows the newest line while the reader is there
  (`use-stick-to-bottom`) and lets go the moment a wheel, a pointer, a touch or a key moves it up.
  After that, the build can write as much as it likes: nothing pulls the reader back down. v1's
  panel yanked the reader to the bottom on every progress tick.
- **Know what is happening below.** While the reader is away, the jump pill says why: "Latest",
  "Building" or "Editing". Pressing it brings them back and puts focus on the newest record's head.
- **Start well.** With no turns, أبو غضب's greeting and four starters sit at the bottom, directly
  above the composer, where the eye already is.

## When to use / not

AgentPanel renders one, as its middle grid row (`1fr`), between AgentHeader and the dock. Key it by
conversation id, so opening another conversation lands at its bottom at once. Do not put another
scroller inside it or around it.

## Behaviour

- **Structure**: a `section` named "Conversation" («المحادثة») holding an `ol` of turns, each an
  `li` keyed by `turnId`, so a plan that becomes its build keeps its node and its focus target.
  After the list, the suggestions.
- **Not a log.** `role="log"` is implicitly live, and the panel has exactly one live region,
  LiveStatus. Nothing in the thread announces itself.
- **Suggestions** show only when nothing runs, the composer is empty and no decision (a ready plan,
  step plan or proposal) is waiting.
- **The pill** shows only while the reader is away from the bottom (more than 70px), and never over
  the focused element: it hides while focus is anywhere in the newest turn, and whenever the focused
  element reaches into the 56px band it floats over, whichever turn that element is in.
- **Jump**: scrolls to the bottom (a spring; instant under reduced motion) and focuses the head of
  the newest record without scrolling it into view, which would pull the reader back up. A person's
  own last message has no head, so the record before it takes focus; with no record at all, the
  thread itself does. Never `body`.
- **Focus never falls off the pill.** If the pill hides while it has keyboard focus (the reader
  wheeled back to the bottom instead of pressing it), focus goes where pressing it would have sent
  it: the newest record's head.
- **Empty**: no list; EmptyState and its starters, pushed to the bottom.
- **Viewport changes**: the scrolling hook observes both the viewport and the content. A growing
  composer follows the latest only while pinned; a reader browsing history keeps their offset.
- **Hidden panels**: zero-size reports are ignored. Reopening restores the reader's position, or
  resumes following the latest when they had been pinned. A resize never opts a released reader back
  in. Browser scroll anchoring is disabled so it cannot compete with this ownership.
- **Input intent**: wheel-up, upward touch movement, and ArrowUp/PageUp/Home/Shift+Space on the
  focused conversation release an active spring before it can overwrite the browser scroll.
  Descendant controls and inner scrollers retain their own input.

## Stories

`Empty`, `EmptyArabic`, `EmptyArabicDark`, `EmptyWithSelection`; `Long`, `LongArabic` (followed to
the bottom); `ScrolledUpLatest`, `ScrolledUpLatestDark`, `ScrolledUpBuilding`,
`ScrolledUpBuildingArabic`, `ScrolledUpEditing`, `ScrolledUpEditingArabicDark`,

reader was not moved, and (`ScrolledUpBuilding`) that jumping lands focus on the newest record's
head.

## Anatomy

```text
┌ thread (1fr, the only scroller) ───────────────┐
│ 12                                             │
│ 16 [turn]                                   16 │   16px between turns
│    [turn]                                      │
│    [▫ suggestion rows]                         │   after the newest settled record
│ 12                                             │
│              ╭────────────╮                    │   the pill: outside the scroller,
│              │ ↓ Building │                    │   centred, 12px above the dock
│              ╰────────────╯                    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- Padding: 12 on the block axis, 16 on the inline axis (12 below a 320px panel).
- `scroll-padding-block-end` keeps keyboard focus above the pill's band when the browser scrolls an
  element into view.
- `scrollbar-gutter: stable`: a scrollbar arriving mid-build never reflows the rows.
- The thread is focusable (`tabIndex=0`), with the product's ring drawn inside its edge, so it
  scrolls from the keyboard even when it holds nothing else that takes focus.

## Motion

The 2026-10-11 live telemetry increment displays each turn's optional `telemetry` through
RunTelemetry below its record. Only allowlisted public lifecycle phases are announced; private
reasoning is never rendered. Counts are actual provider reports, not context estimates. Missing
counts remain unknown, cached replies incur no new model charge, and unmeasured historical records
are unchanged. Growing telemetry follows only a pinned reader.

The scroll follows with the vendored spring; under reduced motion every scroll the hook makes is
instant. The pill's own motion is JumpToLatest's.
