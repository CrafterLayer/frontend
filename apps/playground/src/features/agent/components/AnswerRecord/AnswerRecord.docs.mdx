# AnswerRecord

A words-only answer (SPEC §4.10): a question was asked, and nothing on the page changed.

<Canvas of={AnswerRecordStories.Long} />

## Purpose

When the person asks rather than instructs, the answer is the outcome. So it is the one record
body in Ink rather than Graphite, under the note glyph (three lines of prose), never the Indigo
ink that means "on the page".

## When to use / not

Every `answer` turn (intent `question`). A request that changed the page is an EditRecord or a
BuildRecord, however much it says.

## States

`Short` (no "Show more"), `Long` (folded to six lines; its play opens it), `LongAt420`,
`EnglishInArabicPanel` and `ArabicInEnglishPanel` (the prose keeps its own direction and
language), `CopyAnswer` (the menu hands `copyAnswer` to the caller), with Arabic and dark twins.

## Accessibility

- An `<article>` named "Answer".
- The prose carries `dir` from the majority of its letters (never `dir="auto"`) and `lang` from
  its script; each line takes its own direction by its own letters.
- "Show more" is a real disclosure: `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` on the prose, focus kept on
  it, "Show less" once open. It appears only when six lines hide something, measured on resize.

## RTL

The record mirrors with the panel; the prose follows its own language, so an English answer in
the Arabic panel reads left to right.

## Motion

The disclosure chevron turns over 120ms; nothing else moves. Instant under reduced motion.
