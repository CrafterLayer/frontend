# ClarifyPanel

Questions before guessing: when a request is too unclear to act on, أبو غضب asks one to three short
questions, docked on top of the composer, instead of running the request as it read it.

<Canvas of={ClarifyPanelStories.Asking} />

## Purpose

The owner's own request («هل يمكنك تربط ال صفحه هاي داخل عليها») was taken literally. Now the agent
says what it understood, asks what it must know (which page, where, how it looks), and offers the
likely answers as chips. "Send as written" skips the questions; Cancel puts the request back in the
box, unsent.

## Anatomy

The composer's first section, over a Hairline. It reads like a decision card's head: the Indigo
diamond (RunGlyph `decision`) on the glyph column, the title in Heading type, "1 of 3" in Figure
Slate at the inline end, and every line under it on the record's text column, 24px in. One question
at a time, so the panel stays short in a 320px column. Choices are chips: Tint and Graphite at rest,
Mist and Indigo deep with a check once chosen, 32px tall (44 on a coarse pointer). Send as written is
the secondary button (Rule border), Cancel a ghost.

## Accessibility

- A group named by its title. Progress is drawn "1 of 3" and spoken "Question 1 of 3".
- Each question's chips are a `radiogroup` named by the question; one roving tab stop. A pick
  commits, so the arrows only move: ← → ↑ ↓ in the reading direction, Home and End jump, Enter or
  Space picks.
- Focus moves to the first chip when it opens, to the next question after each answer, and to the
  working line while rewriting; the Composer returns it to the textarea when the panel goes.
- Other opens a field described by its question; Enter sends the answer, Esc returns to the chips.

## RTL

The diamond at the right; ← moves forward. The owner's Arabic questions and chips are authored
Arabic, not mirrored English.

## Motion

Arrives with opacity and a 4px rise over 220ms. The rewriting line shimmers in the reading direction
(SPEC §5 moment 10). Reduced motion: none; the line stays Slate.
