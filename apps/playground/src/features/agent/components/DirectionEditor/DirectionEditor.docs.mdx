# DirectionEditor

The plan's look, editable in place (SPEC §4.11): the mood line, the five colours and the font pair.

<Canvas of={DirectionEditorStories.Default} />

## Purpose

A page's direction is three decisions: how it should feel, which colours it uses, which faces set
it. The editor shows them as the line they are and lets each be changed without leaving the card.
Every change leaves as one `PlanOp` (`setMood`, `setToken`, `setFonts`); the model applies it to the
engine's structured plan, and the card shows the result.

## When to use / not

Use it inside PagePlanCard when the plan carries structured direction (engine E7). Without it, the
card shows a read-only DirectionLine of the direction prose. After Build, the direction is a
DirectionLine in the build record.

## States

| story                                               | what it shows                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| `Default`, `Dark`, `RightToLeft`, `RightToLeftDark` | the mood, five swatches, the pair             |
| `At420`, `At420RightToLeft`                         | the pair's specimen and both faces            |
| `UnreadableText`                                    | a held text colour that fails: the Amber flag |
| `EditingMood`                                       | the field while typing: Sheet and Rule border |

## Anatomy

Two lines. The mood: a 32px text field in Graphite that looks like its line (no box at rest, the
Wash on hover, the Sheet with a Rule border while editing) with a pencil at its inline end. Under
it, the swatches and the pair: five 20px chips in 32px targets (44 on a coarse pointer), 12px apart
(8 between the 44px targets), background, surface, text, accent, text on accent; the FontPairMenu
trigger at the inline end. The field and the swatch row hang into the card's padding by their own
inner space, so the mood's first letter and the first chip's edge sit on the card's content edge.

## Accessibility

- The mood field is named "Style". Enter or leaving it applies; Esc restores the plan's mood; an
  emptied field applies nothing (the mood it held stays).
- The swatches are a group named "Colours"; each is named by its role and code ("Text: #0E1116.
  Change"), and a held colour that fails its pair is described by the verdict.
- The mood is at most 200 characters, the engine's limit on a plan's signature.

## RTL

Logical properties only. The swatches follow the reading direction, background first; the mood takes
its own direction from its script.

## Motion

Colour-only state changes at 120ms. The popover and the menu have their own.
