# HistoryPopover

The Conversations list (SPEC §4.18, §3.13): a Radix Popover hanging from the header's `[◷]` in the
docked panel, and a page of its own in the phone sheet, with Back.

<Canvas of={HistoryPopoverStories.List} />

## Purpose

Every conversation is kept, titled by its first request. The list lets a person go back to one, see
which one is on screen, and let an old one go, without leaving the panel or losing their place in
the thread. It is a decision surface only while it is open: it floats (e2) and closes on Esc, on a
choice, or on a press elsewhere.

## When to use / not

Use it once per panel, with the header's Conversations button as `anchorRef` (AgentHeader's
`conversationsRef`); AgentHeader owns the button's `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. In the phone
sheet pass `presentation="sheet"`.

It renders `SessionSummary[]` and calls the controller (`openConversation`, `deleteConversation`);
it never opens, deletes or sorts anything itself. Page restore points are a different feature
(HistoryPanel), not this list.

## Anatomy

**Popover:** the Sheet with a hairline and the floating shadow (e2), radius 9, 4px inset like every
menu; as wide as the panel less 8px a side (`100cqi − 16`), never wider than 360; never taller than
60% of the panel. The head is the title "Conversations" (Read 600). Above eight conversations a
search field follows (32px, 44 on a finger; Input type, 16px on a finger so iOS never zooms). While
a run is live, one Meta line says why the others wait. Then the rows (HistoryRow), the only part
that scrolls, contained so a scroll never carries on into the thread.

One text edge: the title, the search glyph, the busy line, every row's title and the empty line all
start 12px in.

**Phone sheet:** the list covers the sheet's full face, header and all, on Paper. Its head is the
sheet header's 56px, with a 44px Back where Close stood, and the title in Name type. Rows are 44px
with Delete always visible.

## Accessibility

Keys (SPEC §6.7): ↑ ↓ move between conversations, Home and End jump, Enter opens, Delete (or
Backspace) asks to delete, Esc closes. The rows share one tab stop (a roving tabindex), so Tab goes
from the search field into the list and on out of it. ↓ from the search field enters the list; ↑
from the first row returns to it.

Focus (SPEC §6.3), each destination tested by name:

| after                          | focus goes to                                            |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| opening                        | the search field, else the current conversation          |
| Esc, a choice, Back            | `[◷]` Conversations                                      |
| a press elsewhere              | stays where the person put it                            |
| Delete asked                   | Cancel; Esc or Cancel returns to the row or trash        |
| a delete                       | the row that took its place, else the new last row       |
| a delete that empties a search | the search field                                         |
| a delete that leaves nothing   | New conversation (`focusWhenEmpty`), and the list closes |

The popover is a Radix dialog (non-modal) named by its title; the phone page is a region named the
same. Pressing `[◷]` while the list is open closes it once instead of closing and reopening it. Esc
while a delete question is open answers the question first and leaves the list open. In the phone
sheet Esc goes back to the chat and never reaches the sheet behind it.

## RTL

The list mounts inside the panel (its portal scope is read off `[◷]`), so it takes the panel's
`dir`, `lang` and theme. Logical properties only: in Arabic it hangs from `[◷]`'s left end, rows put
their bar at the right and their time at the left, and Back points right (mirrored). The search
field and every title run in their own script's direction (`unicode-bidi: plaintext`, `resolveDir`).

Search folds what people type interchangeably: hamza carriers and wasla to a bare alef, «ى» to «ي»,
«ة» to «ه», Persian «ی» and «ک», harakat and tatweel dropped, Arabic-Indic digits to Latin, and
Latin case and accents ignored. Every word must appear, in any order.

## Motion

SPEC §5 moment 9: the popover arrives with opacity and a 4px rise from its anchor's side over 220ms
(`enter`, `easing-enter`) and leaves over 140ms (`exit`, `easing-exit`); the phone page arrives the
same way. Under reduced motion, opacity only, 120ms.
