# KnowledgePopover

"Skills & memory" (2026-10-12): what the Agent knows, in one settings window opened from the
header's Skills & memory button: the person's own skills and the default ones, the project memory
and the page's checkpoints. Redesigned after Anthropic's settings (the person's request): a centred
modal over a scrim, a side list of sections with a search box, the section under its own heading,
Close at the inline end. A page of its own in the phone sheet.

<Canvas of={KnowledgePopoverStories.Skills} />

## Purpose

The Agent reads three kinds of standing knowledge on every request: skills the person wrote
(SkillsTab), facts and preferences they asked it to keep (MemoryTab), and the page as it was before
each request (CheckpointsTab). This popover is where a person sees and changes all three without
leaving the conversation. It is modal: the scrim covers the editor, focus stays inside, and Esc,
Close or a press on the scrim closes it and returns focus to the header's button. It mounts inside
the panel's portal scope (its theme, language and direction) and `position: fixed` centres it on the
screen. Under 640px of screen the section list becomes a row of tabs on top.

The search finds a section by its name and its words (`knowledgeLabels(locale).searchWords`), blind
to case, diacritics and the Arabic letter variants people type (ة/ه, أ/ا, ى/ي).

## When to use / not

Once per panel, when the host gives AgentPanel its `knowledge` (`useAgentKnowledge(...).view`).
AgentPanel passes the header's button as `anchorRef` (AgentHeader `knowledgeRef`) and owns its
`aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`; opening it closes the Conversations list, and the other way
round. In the phone sheet pass `presentation="sheet"`.

It renders the view and calls its edits; it never stores anything itself. The Conversations list
(HistoryPopover) and a record's "Restore the page to before this request" (RestoreDialog) are other
surfaces.

## Anatomy

**Popover:** the Sheet with a hairline and the floating shadow (e2), radius 9, as wide as the panel
less 16px and never wider than 400; never taller than 75% of the editor. The head is the title
"Skills & memory" (Read 600) and Close. Under it the tabs, a segmented row on Sunken, the chosen tab
raised on the Sheet. Then the tab's panel, the only part that scrolls, contained so a scroll never
carries on into the thread.

**Phone sheet:** the page covers the sheet's face on Paper, its head as tall as the sheet header.

## Accessibility

- The modal is a Radix dialog named by its title; the phone page is a region named the same.
- The tabs are a `tablist` named by the title, each a `tab` with `aria-selected` and one tab stop
  (roving `tabindex`). → and ← move between them (mirrored in Arabic: ← moves on), Home and End
  jump; the panel is a `tabpanel` named by its tab.
- Opening focuses the chosen tab. Close, Esc and the header button close it and return focus to the
  button; a press elsewhere leaves focus where the person put it. Pressing the button while it is
  open closes it once rather than closing and reopening it.
- In the phone sheet Esc closes the page.

## RTL

It mounts inside the panel (its portal scope is read off the header's button), so it takes the
panel's `dir`, `lang` and theme. Logical properties only: in Arabic it hangs from the button's left
end and the tabs run right to left.
