# MentionMenu

The `@` list (SPEC §4.17, §3.2): the page's sections and blocks by their human names, sections first
with their place on the page ("3 of 8").

<Canvas of={MentionMenuStories.Open} />

## Purpose

Point a request at a block without knowing its id. Picking inserts a chip and removes the `@fragment`
from the text. The pattern accepts letters in any script, and tashkeel, so an Arabic name keeps the
list open mid-word.

## Anatomy

e2: the Sheet, a hairline, the floating shadow, radius 9. Rows are the panel's rows, 32px (44 on a
coarse pointer), at most six in view (four on a coarse pointer), then it scrolls. The active option
is Mist under Ink with a 2px Indigo bar at its inline start; the place is Figure type in Slate,
Graphite on the active option's Mist (Slate is forbidden there).

## Accessibility

- A named listbox of options. The keys belong to the textarea, which stays focused and is the
  combobox: ↑ ↓ move, Enter or Tab pick (never send), Esc closes (SPEC §6.7).
- A pointer press does not take focus from the textarea (mousedown is prevented), so the caret stays.
- The active option scrolls into view.

## RTL

The glyph and name at the right, the place at the left; names in `<bdi>`; digits stay Latin.

## Motion

Opacity and a 4px rise from the composer below it, 220ms in (SPEC §5 moment 9); opacity only under
reduced motion.
