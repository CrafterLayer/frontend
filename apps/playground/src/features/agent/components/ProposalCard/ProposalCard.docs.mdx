# ProposalCard

Ask mode's proposed edit (SPEC §3.8, §4.13): what would change, the promise that nothing has changed
yet, and Apply / Discard. Boxed until it is answered.

<Canvas of={ProposalCardStories.Pending} />

## Purpose

In Ask mode an edit is prepared, never applied, until the person says so. The card shows the change
in the page's own words ("Heading “FAQ” → “Questions patients ask”") and says, only while it is
true, that the page has not changed. Apply writes it; Discard leaves a one-line end note.

It never shows a token or cost estimate. Those belong to telemetry and Technical details (SPEC
§7.4); the owner once saw «تقدير: ~11.3k توكن» on a one-step plan, and that line does not exist
here.

## When to use / not

Use it for a `proposal` turn. While the proposal is being prepared it is a working record.

Do not use it after Apply (the turn becomes an EditRecord titled "Applied: FAQ") or after Discard
(an EndNote). Do not use it for a page request in Ask mode: that is PagePlanCard with its approval
tag.

## Anatomy

The DecisionCard shell (e1, Sheet, Hairline, radius 9, 12px in). The head is the decision diamond
and `proposal.title`, "Proposed change: FAQ". The body starts on the title's edge, 24px in, where
the applied record keeps it: ChangeLines, then the Amber partial line when some changes did not
survive, then the honesty line (Meta, Graphite). The footer: Apply (the Indigo fill), Discard
(Indigo words), and at the inline end the Preview toggle and `[⋯]`.

## Accessibility

- An `<article>` named by its `h3`; the target is a `<bdi>`.
- Preview is a toggle (`aria-pressed`); while pressed it is described by "End preview". Esc inside
  the card ends the preview and focus lands on the toggle.
- Apply waits `aria-disabled` when validation kept nothing.
- Focus names its destination (SPEC §6.3): Apply → the resulting record's Undo; Discard → the
  composer. The card never holds focus through the change: it names the destination and waits for it
  to appear in the same turn.
- No live region of its own: the controller announces through LiveStatus.

## RTL

Logical properties only. The target is quoted «» in Arabic and “” in English by the catalog, and
takes its own `lang` when its script differs from the UI.

## Motion

None of its own. When the decision is answered the turn becomes a record (SPEC §5 moment 7).
