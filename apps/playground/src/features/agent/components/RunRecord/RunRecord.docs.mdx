# RunRecord

The shell every past run is drawn in (SPEC §4.6): BuildRecord, EditRecord, AnswerRecord,
ErrorRecord, and a decision card once it is answered.

<Canvas of={RunRecordStories.EveryKindAt320} />

## Purpose

The panel is an instrument, not a chat app (SPEC §1). Only two things get a border: the composer,
where you write, and a decision waiting for you. Everything that already happened is an unboxed
record: a status glyph, a title, a few precise lines. So what is waiting for you stands apart from
what is history at a glance.

RunRecord is that record: the geometry every record shares, so seven kinds of record line up on one
edge.

## When to use / not

Use it for every turn that reports a run, whether it is running, finished, failed or answered. A
decision still waiting (a page plan, a proposal) is a card with a border, not a record; it becomes
one once answered.

Do not put a box, a fill or a fourth type size in it. Do not show a ticking clock in its head: while
a run is live the RunBar owns the one clock, and the head shows the count.

## States

The stories compose the records wave 2 builds on this shell: `BuildRunning`, `BuildDone` (with an
InlineConfirm for Undo build and a RecordMenu), `BuildPartial`, `BuildStopped`, `Failed`, `EditDone`
(with ChangeLines) and `Answer`; `BuildDoneDark`, `BuildDoneRightToLeft`, `BuildDoneRightToLeftDark`
and `EditDoneRightToLeft`; and every kind stacked at 320 (`EveryKindAt320`) and in Arabic at 420
(`EveryKindAt420Arabic`). `UndoBuildConfirming` and `UndoBuildConfirmingRightToLeftDark` open the
Undo build InlineConfirm. Their plays check that the article is named by its title, that every body
line starts on the title's edge, that nothing scrolls sideways, and, with the confirm open, that
`[⋯]` stays at the footer's inline end on the same row as Undo and Cancel.

The footer buttons in these stories are placeholders at the panel's 32px control height: each
record's own buttons belong to its work package (WP-12).

## Anatomy

```text
[glyph 16] 8 [title, h3, one line …………………………] [figures]      head, 32px
           [body lines, in the text column]                   4px under the head, 2px apart
           [primary] [secondary]                    [⋯]       8px under the body
           [consequence of Undo build, wrapping ……]           an InlineConfirm, open:
           [Undo] [Cancel]                          [⋯]       [⋯] stays on the answers' row
```

- **Head**: a grid of a 16px glyph column, 8px, the title, and the figures at the inline end. The
  title is Heading type (Read, 600) in Ink, one line, ending in an ellipsis. The figures are Figure
  type, tabular, in Slate, each in an LTR `<bdi>`.
- **Body**: a two-column grid, the glyph column and the text column, so every line starts under the
  title, 24px in. A child that draws in the glyph column (a SpineList, a spine segment beside a
  caption) spans both with `grid-column: 1 / -1`. Lines default to Read in Graphite.
- **Footer**: 24px in, 8px under the body, 44px tall on a coarse pointer. Two columns: the actions
  (`.ck-agent-record__actions`), which wrap within their own column with 8px gaps, and `[⋯]` at the
  inline end, on the actions' last row. An InlineConfirm takes a full row of the actions column (its
  consequence, then its answers), and `[⋯]` stays beside the answers. In a single wrapping row it
  dropped to a line of its own, 40px under them (measured in Chromium). A menu-only footer (an
  answer) keeps `[⋯]` at the end.

## Accessibility

- An `<article aria-labelledby>` on its `h3`: a screen reader lists records by their titles.
- The title says the state in words ("Build stopped"); the glyph is `aria-hidden` and adds nothing
  to the name, so no state is carried by colour or shape alone. A test renders every glyph state and
  checks the name is only the title.
- The heading takes `tabindex="-1"`, so an action can send focus to it (Build this page, Retry,
  Finish the rest, Undo build), with the product's ring drawn around the words.
- A figure whose bare text would not make sense read aloud takes a `label`: `1/8` is read as "1 of 8
  sections is on the page".

## RTL

Logical properties only: in Arabic the glyph column is at the right, the figures at the left, and
`2:21` and `1/8` stay intact in their LTR islands. The stories check the glyph sits right of the
figures.

## Motion

None of its own. The glyph's arrival motion runs only when `animateGlyph` is set.
