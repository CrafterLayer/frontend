# SpineRow

One section of the page spine (SPEC §4.8 SpineRow): node, number, title and state word, then one
trailing action. 32px tall, 44px on a coarse pointer.

<Canvas of={SpineRowStories.StateWordsAt420} />

## Purpose

Each row tells the truth about its section: waiting (a Rule ring over dashed pencil), writing (a
Halo ring with a rotating arc, a light travelling down its stretch), written and waiting its
turn (an Indigo outline ring, a still pale stretch), on the page (an Indigo disc with a check,
solid ink), failed (an Alarm disc, a dashed Alarm stretch, the reason on a second line), not
written or undone (a dashed ring over pencil). Activating a landed row selects its section.

## When to use / not

Only inside SpineList, which gives it its columns through subgrid. Plan rows before Build are
PlanSectionRow (WP-13).

| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `locale`           | `Locale`                                    | none    |                                                              |

The module also exports `SpineSegment` (one stretch, drawn with borders), `segmentForRow` and
`trailingActionOf`, which decide once that only a landed row inks the stretch above its node,
and what a row offers at its end.

## States

`Waiting`, `Writing`, `Retrying`, `Drafted`, `Landed` (Undo on hover or focus), `LandedUndoOnFocus`,
`Undone` (Redo), `Failed` (Retry always visible, the reason in Alarm), `NotWritten`,
`UndoUnavailable` (revived: `aria-disabled`, described), `UndoRefused` (the page moved on: the
reason on its own line), `StateWordsAt420`, `AccessibleName`, and Arabic and dark twins.

## Anatomy

```text
[✓] 2  Hero                     On the page    [↺]
node · number (Figure, Slate) · title (Read) · state word (Meta, Slate) · trailing action
       Took too long                                  second line on a failed row (Meta, Alarm)
```

- **Title colour:** Slate while planned, waiting, not written or undone; Ink when writing,
  drafted, landed or failed; weight 500 while a writer is on it, settling to 400 as it lands.
- **State word:** shown from 400px for every state (failed in Alarm); below 400 only where the
  node alone could be mistaken: a second try, not written, undone. Below 400 a failed row says
  why on its second line instead, beside Retry, as the §3.5 frame draws it. Always in the
  accessible name.
- **Trailing action (32px):** Undo on a landed row after `build.completed` (fine pointer: on
  hover or `:focus-within`; coarse: always); Redo on an undone row; Retry, always visible, on a
  failed row.

## Accessibility

- Two sibling interactive elements, never nested: the row itself, a button while its section is
  on the page ("Section 3 of 8: Services, Writing"), and the trailing action ("Undo Hero").
- A section with no node yet is text, not a button, and says the same sentence through a
  visually hidden line.
- Undo and Redo are one button whose label follows the state, so focus stays on it when a row's
  Undo lands (SPEC §6.3).
- A revived Undo is `aria-disabled` (still focusable) and described by the record's caption;
  pressing it does nothing.
- Focus ring from `--bldr-focus-ring`, drawn inside the row (`outline-offset: -2px`).

## RTL

Logical properties only. The number and the state word are the catalog's; titles are `<bdi>`
with their own `lang` when their script is not the panel's.

## Motion

Landing: the stretch fills top-down over 360ms, the node crossfades to the disc and its check
scales 0.6 → 1, the title settles 500 → 400 (§5 moment 1). The light on a writing stretch:
1.4s linear loop (moment 2). A finished draft: the light stops, the ring turns Indigo (120ms,
moment 3). Reduced motion: every state instant, the writing stretch a still Halo line.
