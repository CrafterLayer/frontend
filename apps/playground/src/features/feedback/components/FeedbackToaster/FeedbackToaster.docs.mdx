# FeedbackToaster

The outcome of a person's own action, said once near the canvas and gone on its own. Publishing is
the first action it narrates.

<Canvas of={FeedbackToasterStories.Published} />

## Purpose

A consequential action needs an answer. Before this, Publish site downloaded a zip in silence, and a
failure only changed the button's tooltip. Now one toast carries the whole story: "Publishing the
site…" while it runs, the outcome in place when it lands ("Site published · 5 files", the files are
downloading), and on failure what happened, that nothing was lost, and **Try again**.

## When to use / not

Use it for the outcome of an action the person just took (publish, export, a palette action whose
effect is off screen). Do not use it for background facts (saving, the assistant's progress: they
have their own quiet places in the status bar and the panel), for errors that need a decision (a
dialog), or for anything the person must be able to find again later.

## Props

| prop     | type             | default | notes                                |
| -------- | ---------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------ |
| `labels` | `FeedbackLabels` | English | `feedbackLabels(locale)` picks EN/AR |
| `dir`    | `'ltr' \| 'rtl'` | `'ltr'` | the editor's direction               |

Toasts are raised through `notifyPublish(publish, labels, retry)` in `features/feedback/notify.ts`,
which keeps sonner behind one door.

## States

| story                  | what it shows                                     |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `Published`            | the outcome, with the count and what happens next |
| `Failed`               | what happened, nothing lost, Try again            |
| `Working`              | the quiet running line and its ring               |
| `PublishedRightToLeft` | Arabic, right to left, the count in agreement     |

## Anatomy

A Panel card 352px wide, bottom-centre above the status bar: the Hairline edge, the 12px radius, the
floating elevation. A 28px tile with the outcome's glyph in its colour (Success, Danger, Accent; a
ring while working), the title in Ink, one sentence of detail in Graphite, and on failure Try again
in the Accent wash. Close sits at the inline end (44px on a coarse pointer).

## Accessibility

- sonner's region is a polite live region, named "Notifications" («الإشعارات»).
- Toasts pause while hovered or focused; Alt T moves focus to them; swipe or Close dismisses.
- The spinner stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`; forced colours keep the card's edge.
