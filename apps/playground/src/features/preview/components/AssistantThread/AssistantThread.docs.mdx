# AssistantThread

The conversation itself: every turn, in order, each rendered as the kind of thing it is.

<Canvas of={AssistantThreadStories.Thinking} />

## It dispatches; it does not decide

Every card below owns its own rules — when Apply is withdrawn, when Undo is a write, when Retry
would re-send nothing. This component's whole job is to hand each turn to the card that knows what
to do with it: [PlanCard](?path=/docs/playground-plancard--docs),
[OutcomeCard](?path=/docs/playground-outcomecard--docs),
[BuildReportCard](?path=/docs/playground-buildreportcard--docs),
[BuildSectionList](?path=/docs/playground-buildsectionlist--docs),
[BuildApprovalCard](?path=/docs/playground-buildapprovalcard--docs),
[OutlineCard](?path=/docs/playground-outlinecard--docs) and
[LiveStageClock](?path=/docs/playground-livestageclock--docs).

That is why the props list is wide and shallow: it is a switchboard, and every wire is visible.

## A log, not an announcer

`role="log"` and no `aria-live` of its own. A thread is content appended over time, which a screen
reader should be able to review at its own pace. The panel keeps exactly one live region — the
agent's voice in the header — so a person is never told the same thing twice.

## The empty state is an invitation

<Canvas of={AssistantThreadStories.Empty} />

One sentence saying what to ask for, and his face waiting for it. It disappears the moment there is
a conversation, and it is the only place in the thread that speaks before being spoken to.

## Moods: live above, frozen below

<Canvas of={AssistantThreadStories.Stopped} />

An in-flight bubble — `thinking` or `building` — mirrors the header's live mood. Every settled
bubble is frozen at the mood it earned, from `moodForTurn`. A finished answer that kept changing
expression would be re-litigating a decision already made.

Every avatar is `aria-hidden`: the mood is decoration, and the card beneath it carries the words.

<Canvas of={AssistantThreadStories.Outline} />

## What a person wrote keeps its own direction

Their text is rendered `dir="auto"`, exactly like the provider's. The chrome around it is the only
part with an opinion about direction.
