# BuildApprovalCard

The one question a split build asks.

<Canvas of={BuildApprovalCardStories.Default} />

## Why there is exactly one

A page-sized request is minutes of work and many model calls. The build stops here once — with the
direction it decided, the sections it would write, and two buttons — and then answers its own
failures to the end. A long build that kept interrupting would be worse than a slow one.

The card is a named `group`, so the question is findable rather than being loose text in a log.

## The note is the reason, not reassurance

"Each section is applied as its own change, so any step can be undone on its own" is the fact that
makes Build safe to press. Each section lands as its own transaction, so a person who dislikes one
of six removes that one — they are not choosing between the whole page and nothing.

The skeleton is already on the page by this point, which is why the sentence says so: what is being
approved is the FILLING, not the structure.

## Language

The direction is the scaffold's own words, in the language the request was made in — not the
panel's. `dir="auto"` is what keeps an Arabic direction reading right inside an otherwise English
card.

<Canvas of={BuildApprovalCardStories.Arabic} />

## The count is the page's

Five sections, or two. Nothing here assumes a page size; the list and the count both come from what
the scaffold actually laid down.
