# App Download

A section that sends people to a mobile app: what it does in a few benefits, one button per store, a
rating line, and a screenshot in a drawn phone frame. A CTA is one button; a promo banner has no
store buttons, benefits list or device — this is the block for "get our app".

## Content model

- `eyebrow`, `heading` (default "Everything you need, in your pocket", edited in place), `body`.
- `features`: up to five short benefits, each a check-marked line.
- `stores`: up to three buttons, each `{ icon, kicker?, label, href }`. `icon` is a **generic**
  glyph from the project's lucide set — `phone | play | globe | monitor | download` — and `kicker`
  the small lead-in ("Download on the", «حمّله من»). No store's trademarked badge or logo is drawn;
  the store is named in words.
- `note`: a rating or trust line beside a star.
- `imageSrc` / `imageAlt` and `showDevice = true`: a screenshot inside a token-drawn device frame; a
  drawn placeholder screen until one is chosen; without the device, the image alone.
- `layout: 'split' | 'centered' = 'split'`, `mediaPosition: 'start' | 'end' = 'end'`, and the shared
  section props (`tone` defaults to `light`).

## Layout, direction and accessibility

Split becomes two columns from a 48rem container; the copy always comes first in the reading order
and `mediaPosition: 'start'` moves the device visually with `order`, so it lands on the inline start
— the right in Arabic. Each button is one link whose name is its lead-in and store name ("Get it on
Google Play"); the glyph is `aria-hidden`. On a dark section the buttons turn light (`bg-surface`)
so they never vanish into the ground. The device frame and the glow behind it are decoration.
