# Before / After

Two photos of one subject, stacked in one frame, with a divider a person drags to compare them: a
renovation, a dental or skin treatment, a cleaning service, a photo retouch. Nothing else in the
catalog does this — a Gallery shows photos side by side, an Image shows one.

## Content model

- `eyebrow`, `heading` (default "See the difference", edited in place), `body`.
- `beforeSrc` / `beforeAlt`, `afterSrc` / `afterAlt`: safe sources only (root-relative, http(s), or
  a raster data URL). Until both are chosen the frame draws two token-coloured placeholders.
- `beforeLabel = 'Before'`, `afterLabel = 'After'` (the corner badges), `showLabels = true`,
  `caption` (a `<figcaption>`).
- `start: 0–100 = 50` — where the divider starts;
  `aspect: 'wide' | 'landscape' | 'square' | 'portrait' = 'landscape'` (square and portrait frames
  cap their width so they never tower).
- `sliderLabel = 'Drag to compare before and after'`: the slider's accessible name (Accessibility,
  advanced tab).
- The shared section props: `align`, `tone`, `spacing`, `width`, colour roles, motion, typography,
  gutter, custom classes and `partStyles`.

## Live and published

- **Live** (the canvas, Preview, Storybook): React renders a native `input type="range"`, invisible
  and spanning the frame, so a drag anywhere moves the divider; a decorative handle follows it and
  shows the focus ring. On the editing canvas the range is inert (`tabindex="-1"`, no pointer
  events), so a click selects the photo or the label instead.
- **Published**: the page never hydrates, so the frame carries `data-ck-behavior="compare"` with
  `data-ck-compare-label` and `data-ck-compare-start`, and its two `img` children are exactly what
  the renderer's existing `compare` pack expects. The pack appends the same native range, draws the
  divider line and its own 40px thumb, and moves `--ck-compare`; no new script ships, and the block
  draws no second line beside the pack's. Without script the frame shows the static split at
  `start`.

## Accessibility and direction

The control is a real slider: arrow keys, Home / End and Page keys work, and its value is read as a
percentage (`aria-valuetext`). Both photos keep their alt text; a photo with none is named by its
corner label. The divider sits `--ck-compare` from the **inline start**, and the after photo is
clipped with `clip-path: inset()` from the left in LTR and from the right in RTL (`rtl:` utility) —
so "before" is always on the inline-start side, where a native range (which mirrors under RTL) has
its minimum. The labels mirror with it: before at `start-3`, after at `end-3`.
