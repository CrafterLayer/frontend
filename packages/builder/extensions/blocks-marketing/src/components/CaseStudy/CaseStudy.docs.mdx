# Case Study

One customer's story, told the way a services page or an agency portfolio tells it: who the client
is, the numbers that changed, the challenge / approach / result, an image, and a line from the
client — with a link to the full write-up. A Content Section has no results or quote; Stats has no
story; this block holds the three together so they read as one argument.

## Content model

- `eyebrow` ("Case study"), `client` (a pill beside it), `heading` (edited in place), `body`.
- `metrics`: up to four `{ value, label }` — free text, so "32%", "2×" and "6 weeks" all fit.
- `points`: up to four `{ label, text }` — Challenge, Approach, Result by default.
- `quote`, `quoteAuthor`, `quoteRole`; `imageSrc` / `imageAlt`.
- An optional action (`actionLabel` + `actionHref`), `mediaPosition: 'start' | 'end' = 'end'`, and
  the shared section props.

## Semantics, layout and direction

The results are a `<dl>`: each a term (the label) and its definition (the value), drawn value-first
with `flex-col-reverse` and an accent bar at the inline start (`border-s-2`). The story points are a
second `<dl>`. The quote is a `<figure>` with a `<blockquote>` and a `<figcaption>`, its accent bar
also at the inline start. From a 56rem container the story and the image + quote sit side by side;
`mediaPosition: 'start'` moves the aside to the inline start with `order`, keeping the reading
order. The action's arrow mirrors under RTL.
