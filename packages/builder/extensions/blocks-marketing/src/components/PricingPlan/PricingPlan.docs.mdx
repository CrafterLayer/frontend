# PricingPlan

## Purpose and ownership

Owned by `@craftkit/builder-blocks-marketing`. One plan card inside a
[Pricing](?path=/docs/builder-blocks-pricing--docs) section.

## Why this block exists

`pricing` used to carry its plans as **data** — an array of
`{ name, price, period, features: string[] }` that the section rendered itself. That shape can
express a feature list and nothing else. It could not hold a button, an image, a badge, or a note,
which meant the one thing every real pricing section needs — a call to action inside each card — was
impossible to author.

It was a template wearing a builder block's clothes.

A plan is now a real node with a real slot. The card owns its name, its price, and its emphasis;
everything **inside** it is composed.

## Props

| Prop                      | Type                          | Default    | Notes                      |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------- |
| `name`                    | `string` (1–40)               | `'Plan'`   | Required. Inline-editable  |
| `price`                   | `string` (1–24)               | `'$0'`     | **Text, not a number**     |
| `period`                  | `'once' \| 'month' \| 'year'` | `'month'`  |                            |
| `emphasis`                | `'normal' \| 'featured'`      | `'normal'` |                            |
| `background`, `textColor` | colour role                   | —          | Optional                   |
| `motion`                  | `MotionSpec`                  | —          | From the closed vocabulary |

**`price` is text on purpose.** Prices are written as people read them — `$29`, `٢٩ د.أ`, `Free`,
`Contact us`. A numeric field would force a currency decision into the schema that belongs to the
copy.

## Slots

`default` accepts `stack`, `grid`, `richText`, `cta`, `image`, `navLinks`, and `features`.

`pricingPlan` is absent from its own list: a plan inside a plan is not a layout anyone means, and
permitting it costs a person a confusing drop target for no gain.

## Two design decisions

**Featured is outlined, not repainted.** A card painted in the accent competes with the button
inside it, and the reader then has two things shouting and no idea which to press. Emphasis is a
border and a shadow.

**The body grows.** `flex-1` on the composed area is what keeps a row of cards with unequal content
aligned at the footer — the detail that separates a real pricing table from three boxes of different
heights.

## Styling

Entirely token-bound Tailwind utilities; this block ships **no stylesheet**. `bg-surface` compiles
to `background-color: var(--t-surface)`, and `p-6` to `calc(var(--t-space-unit) * 6)` — six steps of
the site's own rhythm, not twenty-four pixels. Re-point one token and every card moves.

## Accessibility

The name is an `<h3>`, so a screen reader can list the plans. The price and its period share one
paragraph, so they announce as `"$29 per month"` rather than as two unrelated fragments.

The card is an `<article>`: a self-contained unit a reader can navigate to.

## Words and alignment

`badge?` (shown on the featured card) and `periodLabel?` replace the English defaults "Popular" and
"per month"/"per year"/"one-time", so an Arabic plan reads «الأكثر طلبًا» and «شهريًا». Both are
parts (`badge`, `period`). The card's header is start-aligned whatever the section's alignment, so
the name and the price share one axis; only the featured card reserves room for its badge.

## Known limitations

- **It must live inside a pricing section** to be laid out as a column of a row; rendered elsewhere
  it is simply a card.
- **Emphasis has two levels.** A third would need a name nobody has yet asked for.

## Parts

Each inner element is a part (block-parts DESIGN §1): selectable and editable on the canvas, styled
per part through `partStyles`, and addressable by the agent by name, alias or label. Text and link
parts carry `dir="auto"`, so an English line on an Arabic page keeps its full stop at the end. Part
markers (`data-ck-part`, `data-ck-bind`) exist on the canvas only.

| name    | kind | bind    | label (en / ar)       |
| ------- | ---- | ------- | --------------------- |
| `name`  | text | `name`  | Plan name / اسم الخطة |
| `price` | text | `price` | Price / السعر         |
