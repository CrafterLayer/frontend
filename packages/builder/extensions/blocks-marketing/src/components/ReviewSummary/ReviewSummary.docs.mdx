# Review Summary

The aggregate view of what customers say: the average score with stars, how many reviews it rests
on, the share of reviews at each star level, and a few of the reviews. Testimonials shows chosen
quotes; this block shows the whole distribution, the way a store or a clinic's review page does.

## Content model

- `eyebrow`, `heading` (default "What our customers say", edited in place), `body`.
- `average: 0–5 = 4.7` (printed with one decimal), `totalLabel` ("Based on 1,248 reviews").
- `breakdown`: up to five `{ label, count }` rows, top level first. Shares are computed from the
  counts, so the bars always add up.
- `reviews`: up to six `{ quote, author, meta?, rating: 1–5 = 5 }`.
- `numberLanguage: 'en' | 'ar' = 'en'` — Arabic-Indic digits for the average and the shares.
- `ratingLabel = '{rating} out of 5 stars'`: what a screen reader hears for a row of stars
  (Accessibility, advanced tab).
- An optional action (`actionLabel` + `actionHref`), and the shared section props.

## Accessibility and direction

The average is printed large but `aria-hidden`; the stars beside it are one `role="img"` named from
`ratingLabel` ("4.7 out of 5 stars"), so the number is heard once. Each level reads as its label and
its printed share ("5 stars 79%"); the bar beside them is decoration. Each review is a `<figure>`
with a `<blockquote>` and a `<figcaption>`, its own stars named the same way. The bars use
`inline-size`, so they grow from the inline start; the partial star is mirrored under RTL so it
fills from the right in Arabic. The summary card keeps its light ground on a dark section.
