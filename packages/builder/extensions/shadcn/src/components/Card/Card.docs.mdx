# Card

A surface that groups related content. Adapted from shadcn/ui.

<Canvas of={CardStories.WithHeaderAndFooter} />

## What "adapted from shadcn/ui" means here

shadcn/ui is not a dependency — it is a set of components you copy into your own tree and own
outright, which is the same convention this workspace already follows for Aceternity and Magic UI.
There is nothing to install.

Upstream's Card is `rounded-xl border bg-card text-card-foreground shadow`. Three of those four
resolve through **shadcn's own theme contract** — `--card`, `--card-foreground`, `--border` — which
is a second design system living beside this one. A page half in `--card` and half in `--t-surface`
is two products in one document. They are re-pointed at roles this site can actually move.

The shape, the spacing rhythm, and the header/body/footer split are kept.

## The regions are slots, not props

A card whose body is a string can hold a sentence. The reason a card exists is to hold a heading, a
paragraph, an image, **and a button** — so all three regions take blocks.

This is the same defect the Pricing section had before it was split into a container and a card: a
shape that could express a list of strings and nothing else, so the one thing every real pricing
card needs was impossible to author.

## Why the body grows

`flex-1` on the body region is what keeps a row of cards lining their footers up. Without it, a card
with two lines of text sits short and the row reads as broken.

| Prop                 | Meaning                                                                                                |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `tone`               | Ground **and** ink together — a card that sets one and inherits the other is how dark-on-dark happens. |
| `padding` · `radius` | Named steps of the site's own scale.                                                                   |
| `bordered`           | A hairline in the accent role.                                                                         |
| `elevation`          | `soft` / `strong` are the two shadow **tokens** this theme defines.                                    |
