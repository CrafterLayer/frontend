# Background video

`data-ck-behavior="bg-video"` on a `section` (or a `header`, `div` or `figure`) whose first child is
a `<video muted loop playsinline preload="metadata" aria-hidden="true">` laid behind the content. The
`bg-video` pack (`behaviors/packs/bg-video.ts`, `mountBgVideo`) decides when it plays.

<Canvas of={BgVideoStories.English} />

## When it plays

- The tree carries no `autoplay`. An authored one is dropped while the pack is mounted.
- The pack calls `play()` only when four things hold: the page is interactive, motion is allowed,
  the section is in view and the tab is visible. Otherwise it pauses the video.
- Without the script, the poster shows.

## The toggle

A pause/play button sits at the inline end and block end of the section, so it lands bottom left
on an RTL page. It is labelled «إيقاف الخلفية» / «تشغيل الخلفية» on Arabic pages and "Pause
background" / "Play background" on others, so moving content can always be stopped (WCAG 2.2.2).
Once the reader pauses, scrolling back into view does not restart the video.

<Canvas of={BgVideoStories.Arabic} />

## Reduced motion and the canvas

Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and on the editing canvas (`interactive: false`), nothing
plays and no button is drawn. The section is marked `data-ck-bg-video="static"` or `"still"`.

<Canvas of={BgVideoStories.Canvas} />

## Accessibility

The video is decorative (`aria-hidden`), so the content in front of it carries the meaning. The
toggle is a real `button` with a label in the page language and a focus-visible ring.
