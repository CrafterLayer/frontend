# Embed facade

`data-ck-behavior="embed-facade"` on an `<a href>` with a poster image. The link can point at:

- a YouTube video: `youtube.com/watch?v=ID`, `youtu.be/ID` or `youtube.com/shorts/ID`;
- a Vimeo video: `vimeo.com/N`;
- a Google Maps place: `?q=`, `/maps/place/…` or `/@lat,lng`.

Without the script it is a plain link. With the `embed-facade` pack (`behaviors/packs/embed-facade.ts`,
`mountEmbedFacade`), the page still makes no request to the player until the reader asks.

## On click

A click, or Enter on the focused link, swaps the poster for the player:

1. The pack derives the embed address. YouTube becomes
   `https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/ID?autoplay=1`, Vimeo becomes
   `https://player.vimeo.com/video/N?autoplay=1`, and Maps becomes
   `https://www.google.com/maps?q=…&output=embed`.
2. It re-tests that address against inline copies of the schema's iframe allowlist
   (`CUSTOM_CODE_IFRAME_SOURCES`).
3. It builds the iframe with the same `sandbox`, `allow` and `referrerpolicy` the renderer forces on
   every iframe, plus `allowfullscreen`.
4. The link's own children are hidden, not removed, so a host such as React still owns them. Focus
   moves into the iframe.

`embed-facade-parity.test.ts` holds the inline copies equal to the schema's constants.

A modified click (Ctrl, ⌘ or Shift) keeps the link's own behaviour and opens the page in a new tab or
window.

## Title

The iframe's `title` comes from the link's `aria-label`, then from the poster's `alt`. If neither is
set, it falls back to «فيديو» / «خريطة» on Arabic pages and "Video" / "Map" on others.

## On the canvas

With `interactive: false` nothing is wired, so a click selects. The link is marked
`data-ck-facade="still"`.

<Canvas of={EmbedFacadeStories.Canvas} />

## Accessibility

The facade is a real link with a label in the page language («شغّل: …»), so it reads and works
without the script. After the swap, focus lands in the player. Dispose removes the iframe and
restores the link's children and attributes.
