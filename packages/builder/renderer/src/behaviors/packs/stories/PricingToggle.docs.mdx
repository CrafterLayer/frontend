# Pricing toggle

`data-ck-behavior="toggle-group"` on a container whose descendants carry `data-ck-option="a"` (monthly)
or `data-ck-option="b"` (yearly). The markup itself ships the `b` options with `hidden`, so a page without
the script shows the monthly prices and nothing else.

<Canvas of={PricingToggleStories.English} />

## The switch

The `toggle-group` pack (`behaviors/packs/toggle-group.ts`) adopts as its switch the first `button` in
the container that is not inside an option. A Buy button inside a price card is never taken. If the
container has no such button, the pack prepends one. The switch gets:

- `role="switch"` and `aria-checked`;
- the label «شهري / سنوي» or "Monthly / Yearly", but only when the button has no text of its own.

Pressing it flips `hidden` between the `a` and `b` options. The disposer restores every attribute it set,
`hidden` included, and removes a switch it inserted.

## Direction

The track and knob are the switch's `::before` and `::after` (`[data-ck-switch]` in
`WIDGETS_PACK_CSS`), and both are placed with `inset-inline-start` only. When the switch is checked, only
the knob's `inset-inline-start` changes. On an Arabic page the knob therefore starts at the right and
travels left without a separate RTL rule. The knob slides only under
`prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.

## On the canvas

The `a` options show and the switch is inert, so a click selects the element.
