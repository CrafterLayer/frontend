# Video player

`data-ck-behavior="video-player"` on a `figure` (or a `div`) that wraps a native `<video controls>`. The
`video-player` pack (`behaviors/packs/video-player.ts`, `mountVideoPlayer`) draws CraftKit's own
chrome over the video. Without the script, the browser's native controls are the player.

<Canvas of={VideoPlayerStories.English} />

## The chrome

- A centred big play button, and a control bar holding: play/pause, a native `input[type=range]`
  scrubber, the time (`0:12 / 1:30`), mute, a captions toggle (only when the video has a `<track>`),
  picture-in-picture (only where the document supports it) and fullscreen on the wrapper.
- While the pack is mounted, the native `controls` attribute is removed. Dispose puts it back in its
  original place.
- Icons are CSS masks from `VIDEO_PACK_CSS`, so the serialised runtime carries no SVG.

## Keyboard

With focus anywhere in the wrapper:

- Space or K plays and pauses.
- ← and → seek 5 seconds. Under RTL they are swapped, so → goes back.
- M mutes, F toggles fullscreen and C toggles captions.

Letter keys are also read from `event.code`, so they work on an Arabic keyboard layout. The scrubber
keeps its native arrow keys.

## Arabic and RTL

- Labels follow the page `lang`. Arabic gives «تشغيل», «إيقاف مؤقت», «كتم الصوت», «إلغاء الكتم»,
  «ملء الشاشة», «الترجمة», «صورة داخل صورة», «تقديم» and «الوقت».
- Arabic pages write the time in Arabic-Indic digits (`٠:١٢ / ١:٣٠`). The Maghreb regions keep
  Latin digits.
- The bar uses logical properties, and the range input mirrors natively, so nothing is flipped
  twice.

## Playback rules

- The video pauses when it leaves the viewport and when the tab is hidden. Picture-in-picture keeps
  playing.
- Under reduced motion nothing is forced: an authored `autoplay` is dropped, and the reader presses
  play.

## On the canvas

With `interactive: false`, the chrome is drawn but `inert` with `pointer-events: none`, so a click
selects the element. The video never plays there, and the designer sees the real look.

## Accessibility

Every control is a real `button` with a label in the page language. Captions, picture-in-picture
and fullscreen report their state with `aria-pressed`. The scrubber carries `aria-valuetext` with the
spoken time, and the time display is a `timer`. Each control shows a focus-visible ring.
